# Section 1: Careers Page Editor

The **Careers Page Editor** is your main workspace for designing candidate landing pages. It combines a live, interactive preview on the left/center with intuitive control panels on the right and top.

---

## 1.1 Section Management

Your careers page is built out of modular **sections** (like Hero, About Us, Company Culture, Perks & Benefits, Leadership Team, Testimonials, FAQs, Call to Action, and Photo Gallery). 

Here is how you control them:

### ⬆️ ⬇️ Reordering Sections
- **How it works**: Hover over any section card in the left sidebar (or click the up/down arrows on the canvas toolbar).
- **What happens**: The section moves up or down in order. The live preview updates immediately to reflect the new vertical layout order.

### ➕ Adding New Sections
- **How to use**: Click the **"➕ New Section"** tab or the **"Add Section"** button.
- **Available Sections**:
  - `Hero`: The main headline, hero image background, badge, and call-to-action button.
  - `About`: Company mission story, key milestones, and embedded YouTube intro video.
  - `Culture`: Core values grid with custom icons and descriptions.
  - `Perks`: Employee benefits grid (health, remote stipends, learning budgets, equity).
  - `Team`: Leadership & key team member bio cards.
  - `Testimonials`: Quotes and avatars from current employees.
  - `FAQ`: Accordion list answering candidate questions.
  - `CTA`: Bottom banner encouraging candidates to view open roles.
  - `Gallery`: Photo collage of company offsites, culture, and office life.

### 👁️ Hiding Sections (Toggle Visibility)
- **How it works**: Click the **Eye icon (Toggle Visibility)** on any section card.
- **Purpose**: Want to prepare a seasonal section (like a holiday event or special perk) without showing it to candidates yet? Hiding a section keeps all your written content intact in the editor while taking it off the live candidate site.

### 📋 Creating Copies (Duplicating Sections)
- **How it works**: Click the **Duplicate icon**.
- **Purpose**: Creates an exact clone of the section (including all titles, content, and list items). This is super handy if you want to create multiple custom culture or perk sections without typing everything from scratch!

### 🗑️ Deleting Sections
- **How it works**: Click the **Trash icon**.
- **Safety Guarantee**: The editor asks for a quick confirmation so you don't accidentally delete your work.

---

## 1.2 Live Selection & Real-Time Sync

We designed the editor so you never have to guess how your page will look:

1. **Click to Select**: Whenever you click on any section card in the sidebar (or click directly on a section inside the live preview canvas), that section becomes **Active**.
2. **Auto-Scroll to Preview**: Selecting a section automatically smooth-scrolls the live preview on the center/left right to that section!
3. **Right Inspector Panel**: The right-hand panel instantly displays all editable fields for that specific section (titles, subtitles, main story text, list items, statistics, and icons).
4. **Live Typing Sync**: As you type or change text in the right inspector panel, you will see the letters and styles update **live on the preview screen in real time**!

## 1.4 Branding Tools & Fallbacks

The **Branding Tab** lets you customize your employer identity.

### Tools Available:
- **Company Name**: Your public brand name.
- **Logo URL**: Link to your company logo PNG/SVG image.
- **Banner / Hero Image URL**: High-resolution cover photo for the hero section background.
- **Company Tagline & Mission Description**: Short elevator pitch explaining what your company builds.
- **Website URL**: Direct link to your corporate homepage.
- **YouTube Intro Video URL**: Link to a video (e.g., `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...`).
- **Social Media Links**: LinkedIn, Twitter/X, GitHub, Glassdoor, and Instagram URLs.

### 🛡️ What Happens If You Leave Fields Empty? (Smart Fallbacks)
You never have to worry about broken layouts if you skip a column or field:
- **No Logo URL?**: The header automatically renders a stylized text logo using your company name and primary brand color!
- **No Hero Banner URL?**: The hero background gracefully falls back to a dark gradient background with a subtle noise texture overlay.
- **No YouTube Video URL?**: The About section automatically expands the written story column to a comfortable, readable single-column width without leaving empty white space!
- **Empty Social Links?**: Only social links that contain a URL will render their respective icon in the footer. Unfilled social links stay hidden so your footer always looks clean and complete.

## 1.5 Top Bar & Canvas Controls

At the top of the editor studio, you have top-bar controls to manage your editing session:

- **↩️ Undo & ↪️ Redo**: Made a mistake or want to revert a text edit? Use Undo to step back through your edit history stack, or Redo to re-apply changes.
- **🖥️ 📱 Viewport Mode Switcher**:
  - **Desktop View (`1280px`)**: Full desktop monitor layout preview.
  - **Tablet View (`768px`)**: Simulates iPad/tablet screen width.
  - **Mobile View (`375px`)**: Simulates iPhone/mobile screen width to ensure your careers page is 100% mobile-responsive!
- **💾 Save as Draft**: Saves all your latest edits, colors, section ordering, and content to the database without publishing them to candidates yet.
- **🚀 Publish**: Pushes your draft changes live to the public candidate site in one click!
- **🔗 Open Live Page / Public Link**: Opens your live public careers page (`http://localhost:3000/companies/YOUR-SLUG`) in a new browser tab so you can test the candidate experience firsthand!

# Section 2: Job Management

The **Job Management** module allows recruiters to create, organize, filter, and manage open job requisitions.

---

## 2.1 Role Dashboard & Overview

Recruiters can view all open roles for their company at a glance:
- **Metrics Bar**: Total jobs count, active published roles, draft roles, and department breakdowns.
- **Filtering & Search**: Quickly find any job by searching keywords in the title, department, location, or workplace policy.

---

## 2.2 Creating & Editing Job Postings

Click **"➕ Post New Job"** or click **"Edit"** on any existing job card to open the Job Modal.

### Job Fields Explained:
1. **Job Title**: e.g. *Senior Frontend Engineer*, *Product Manager*, *Account Executive*.
2. **Department**: Category (e.g., *Engineering*, *Product*, *Design*, *Sales*, *Marketing*, *Customer Success*).
3. **Location**: Physical office location or *Remote*.
4. **Workplace Policy**: Choose between **Remote**, **Hybrid**, or **On-site**.
5. **Employment Type**: Choose **Full time**, **Part time**, **Contract**, or **Internship**.
6. **Experience Level**: Choose **Entry-level**, **Mid-level**, **Senior**, or **Lead / Director**.
7. **Salary Range**: e.g. *$120,000 - $150,000 USD / year*.
8. **Job Description**: Detailed summary of the role, responsibilities, and team impact.
9. **Requirements**: Bullet-pointed list of skills, qualifications, and experience expected from applicants.

---

## 2.3 Publishing, Duplicating & Bulk Actions

### 🟢 Published vs 🟡 Draft Status
- **Published**: The job appears live on the candidate careers page and is searchable by job seekers.
- **Draft**: The job is hidden from candidate search while recruiters prepare the job description.

### 📋 Duplicate Job Posting
- Click **"Duplicate"** on any job posting to clone its department, requirements, salary range, and description into a new draft role. Great for hiring multiple engineers or account managers!

### ⚡ Bulk Actions
- Select multiple checkboxes in the job management table to **Bulk Publish**, **Bulk Draft**, or **Bulk Delete** roles in one single action.

---

## 2.4 Candidate Search & Google Jobs SEO

### Candidate Multi-Facet Search
On the public careers page, job seekers get:
- **Instant Keyword Search**: Search titles, departments, or locations with live debounced filtering.
- **Multi-Select Filter Chips**: Filter simultaneously by Department, Workplace Policy, Employment Type, and Location.
- **Interactive Job Modal**: Candidates can click any job card to read the full description and requirements without losing their place on the page!

### 🔍 Search Engine Optimization (Google Jobs)
Every published job posting automatically generates **Schema.org `JobPosting` JSON-LD structured data**. This means your open positions are structured for indexing by **Google Jobs**, LinkedIn, and search engines out-of-the-box!

- **Forgot your password?**: Contact your system administrator or use the demo credentials (`recruiter@workable.com` / `password123`).
